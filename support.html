Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?

You can sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email and password. Use the same method each time so you return to the same account and keep your progress.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the log-in screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your email and tap Send code. We'll email you a one-time code. Enter it in the App, tap Continue, and choose a new password. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder, or tap Resend email. Codes expire after a short time, so use the newest one.

If you signed up with Apple or Google, you don't have a password with us. Just use the same Apple or Google button again.

I signed in with Apple and chose "Hide My Email". Is that OK?

Yes. Apple gives us a private relay address that forwards to your real inbox, and your account works normally. If you contact support, mention that you use Hide My Email so we can find your account.

Is my progress saved if I change phones?

Yes. Your ELO rating, statistics and quest progress are stored with your account, not on the device. Sign in with the same method on your new phone and everything will be there.

Who can see my username and rating?

Your username and ELO rating/score appear on the in-app leaderboard, visible to other signed-in players. Your email address and detailed statistics are private. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does the App involve real money?

No. Free GTO Trainer is a training tool. There is no real-money gambling, wagering or prizes.

How do I delete my account?

In the App, go to your Profile, tap Delete account, then Delete permanently. This permanently removes your account and all associated data, including your username, ELO rating, statistics and quest progress. It cannot be undone.

Can't access the App? Email barak.dekel2002@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it.

Sign in with Apple or Google isn't working.

Make sure you're connected to the internet and that you're signed in to your Apple ID (Settings → your name) or Google account on the device. Then fully close and reopen the App and try again. If it still fails, email us with a screenshot of any error message.
