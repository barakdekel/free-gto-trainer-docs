Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Free GTO Trainer (the "App"), developed by Barak Dekel ("we", "us"), collects, uses and protects your information. The short version:

  • We collect only what the App needs to run your account and save your training progress.
  • We do not use advertising, ad identifiers, third-party analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time, directly in the App.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email and password: your email address and a password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see them in plain text.
  • Sign in with Apple: a unique Apple user identifier, the email address Apple shares with us (this may be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"), and your name if you choose to share it.
  • Sign in with Google: a unique Google account identifier and the basic profile information Google provides, such as your name, email address and profile picture URL.
  • Username: the display name you choose, or one generated for you.

Training and progress data

To track your improvement, the App stores data about your training sessions, including your answers to training hands, per-scenario accuracy statistics, your ELO rating and score, streaks, and daily quest progress, along with the dates and times of this activity.

Technical information

Our hosting provider automatically processes standard technical data when the App communicates with our servers, such as IP address, request timestamps and error logs. This is used only to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service. We also record the time of certain actions to prevent abuse (rate limiting).

What we do not collect

The App does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data, and does not collect advertising identifiers.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To save and sync your training progress, statistics, ELO rating and quests.
  • To show the leaderboard (see section 3).
  • To send account emails you request, such as password reset links.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Information visible to other users

The App includes a leaderboard. Your username and your ELO rating / score are visible to other signed-in users of the App. Your email address, name and detailed training statistics are never shown to other users. If you do not want a recognizable username on the leaderboard, choose one that does not identify you.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the App, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Supabase: provides our database, authentication and account email delivery. Your data is stored on Supabase's servers in India (Asia Pacific, Mumbai region).
  • Apple and Google: only if you choose to sign in with them, to verify your identity.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or security of our users or the service.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time:

  1. Open the App and go to your Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account, then Delete permanently.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all associated data (profile, username, ELO rating, statistics and quest progress) from our database. Residual copies may remain in our hosting provider's routine backups for a limited period before they are overwritten. If you cannot access the App, email us at barak.dekel2002@gmail.com from your account's email address and we will delete your account for you.

If you signed in with Apple or Google, you can also remove the App's access in your Apple ID settings or Google Account connections.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise most of these rights directly in the App; for anything else, contact us at the email below and we will respond within the time required by applicable law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Database access is restricted so that each user can only read and modify their own private data, and sign-in tokens are stored on your device in the system's secure storage (iOS Keychain). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. No real-money gambling

Free GTO Trainer is an educational poker strategy trainer. It does not offer real-money gambling, wagering or prizes, and collects no payment information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will provide notice in the App.
